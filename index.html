About & Disclaimer

LAW & JUSTICE AI is designed to make legal learning and research more accessible across countries and languages.

What this platform can help with

Legal education, plain-language explanations, study planning, issue spotting, research organisation and first-draft structures.

What it does not replace

It is not a law firm, lawyer, court, regulator or official legal database. It does not create a lawyer-client relationship and cannot guarantee that an answer is complete, current or correct.

Global law requires jurisdiction

Always identify the country, state/province and relevant date. Laws, court powers, limitation periods and procedures differ between jurisdictions and can change.

Privacy

This first version stores saved research notes in your current browser using local storage. Do not enter confidential client information or sensitive personal data. Clearing browser data may remove saved items.

Sources

Prefer official legislation websites, court websites, treaty databases and recognised law reports. Treat unsourced claims and AI-generated citations as unverified.
